# Eshoeatelier

Update the branding of the existing luxury footwear e-commerce website.

New official brand name: E'SHOE ATELIER

Tagline: Elegance in Every Step.

Replace every instance of “E'SHOE PRIVÉ” with “E'SHOE ATELIER” throughout the entire project, including:

Homepage and floating-sneaker hero section.

Background wordmark and logo.

Navigation, buttons, and page transitions.

Shoebox animation and packaging-inspired visuals.

Brand story and 2011–2026 timeline.

Product catalogue, product pages, and shopping cart.

Admin dashboard and authentication screens.

Customer emails, abandoned-cart messages, and order confirmations.

Wishlist, reviews, order tracking, and checkout.

Website metadata, browser title, favicon, and social-sharing previews.

Legal pages, policies, and third-party reseller disclaimer.

Preserve the existing luxury aesthetic: obsidian black, champagne gold, warm ivory, and deep espresso, with elegant editorial typography, cinematic animations, the creative cursor, and the floating sneaker.

The brand should feel like an exclusive designer footwear atelier: sophisticated, understated, contemporary, and timeless.

Use the exact spelling E'SHOE ATELIER consistently. Update existing references without unnecessarily rebuilding the website, changing database records, breaking routes, or   .removing functionality. 

Keep all previously specified features, including secure administrator access, product image management, automatic percentage discounts, size guides, wishlists, low-stock alerts, abandoned-cart recovery, genuine customer reviews, order tracking, multi-currency display, and the South African legal requirements.

Do not invent trademark registration, official brand partnerships, or legal approvals. Update the existing project and verify that the new brand identity appears consistently across desktop and mobile.
